About the company

A dependable computational partner for biosciences research.

MolecuNova Computational Biosciences converts biological and chemical data into reliable, interpretable results. We support researchers, laboratories and students with documented, reproducible workflows from raw data to manuscript-ready output.

Mission

To accelerate drug discovery and disease research by turning complex molecular data into validated, decision-ready findings.

Approach

Every engagement follows a documented pipeline with independent validation through statistics, docking, molecular dynamics and DFT where relevant.

Standards

Version-controlled Python, R and Bash workflows on Linux, handed over so that results can be re-run, audited and defended in peer review.

How an engagement works

01 ScopeDefine the research question, data sources and deliverables.
02 AnalyzeBuild the pipeline in Python, R or Galaxy with full documentation.
03 ValidateCross-check with statistics, docking, MD and DFT where relevant.
04 DeliverFigures, code and a written report ready for a thesis or manuscript.
