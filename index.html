Featured research — final year project

🧪 ML-driven S6K1 inhibitor discovery

IOMM Lab, Government College University Faisalabad · 2025 – 2026

A drug-discovery pipeline integrating XGBoost, Random Forest, and SVM models with chemoinformatics and structural bioinformatics to design and screen novel S6K1 inhibitors with therapeutic potential across metabolic disorders and cancer — narrowing a large phytochemical library down to a single validated lead compound via virtual screening, ADMET filtering, and explicit-solvent molecular dynamics.

  • 96.5% / 0.991classification accuracy / AUC-ROC across 8 benchmarked ML classifiers on 2,088 actives and 5,000 decoys
  • 15,000 → 3compounds narrowed via XGBoost-based virtual screening, Lipinski/ADMET filtering, and molecular docking
  • −34.17 kcal/molMM-GBSA binding free energy for Baicalein, the top lead, validated by 100 ns MD in GROMACS
  • In preparationfull-length manuscript for submission to a peer-reviewed computational biology journal
